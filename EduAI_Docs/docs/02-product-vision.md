# Product Vision

## Vision Statement

EduAI is a modern AI-powered digital education platform for learning, teaching, collaboration, and certificate verification.

## Product Positioning

EduAI MVP is best described as:

Google Classroom + Notion Library + ChatGPT Tutor + Jitsi Live Classroom + QR Certificate System

## Core Product Pillars

1. Digital Identity
2. Digital Learning
3. Live Classroom
4. Digital Library
5. Learning Community
6. AI Assistant
7. Digital Certificate

## Competitive Advantage

EduAI does not compete only by having many courses.

EduAI competes through:

- AI-assisted learning
- Integrated live classroom
- Unified learning profile
- Built-in digital library
- QR-verifiable certificates
- Clean modern user experience

## MVP Product Goal

Within 50 days, deliver a production-ready web platform where:

- Instructors can teach.
- Students can learn.
- AI can assist.
- Certificates can be verified.

## Future Product Roadmap

Maximum product roadmap: 100-120 days.

### Phase 1: Day 1-50

MVP Learning Platform.

### Phase 2: Day 51-80

Career Hub, Mentor System, AI Learning Path.

### Phase 3: Day 81-100/120

Marketplace, Payment, Affiliate, Advanced Analytics.

## Non-Goals

EduAI is not:

- A university ERP
- A school administration system
- A tuition system
- A government education platform
- A Zoom clone
- A fully self-trained AI model
