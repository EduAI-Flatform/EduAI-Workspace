# GDS card service limits

Status: IN_PROGRESS. Owner clarification on 2026-11-13: implement service limits first, not course-benefit grants. Member, Golden Member and Platinum Member are initial tiers; Admin may add additional tiers. Annual qualification uses verified eligible spend or top-up facts. No card/package sale is introduced.

## Contract and implementation boundaries

- Extend the existing versioned membership policy and existing service entitlement definitions/grants/usage, rather than introducing another balance or money ledger. No business quota, new service code or threshold is activated by deployment.
- Admin configures typed metered-service limits for each tier using existing stable service definitions. Quotas are bounded positive integer strings, not floating-point amounts. Duplicate service definitions within a tier and mismatched value/reset types are rejected.
- Keep rank qualification and service-limit periods independent. Respect existing definition reset types: lifetime (`none`), calendar month, or a configured fixed term. Fixed terms require an explicit Admin anchor and duration; never use a moving rolling-qualification timestamp as the quota reset key. No monthly assumption is inferred from annual card qualification.
- Membership policy extensions remain compatible with historical revisions lacking limit fields. Missing fields mean no card service limits. Validation/normalization must not rewrite persisted revisions, approval records or prior source digests.
- The normal draft/preview/approve/effective-date workflow applies. Preview distinguishes tier, descriptive-benefit and executable service-limit changes and counts already-consumed usage in projected windows. Enablement requires verified schema/runtime capability; disabled configurations remain valid and inert.
- Resolve qualification from canonical source facts in the same transaction/snapshot as the effective revision. No unverified receipt or second counting of purchases/top-ups qualifies a card. Bounded source-scan failures do not invent a tier or disturb purchased grants.
- Persist card grant provenance: policy revision, tier code, qualification source digest and evaluation/window facts. Repeated resolution cannot refresh the same period or create duplicate grants. Usage references its immutable grant, preserving policy attribution after later changes.
- Pool card-service consumption by user and stable service definition across card tiers/revisions within the configured usage window. A tier upgrade or policy edit must not reset consumption or add old and new quotas together. Recorded usage stays immutable and counts even when an older grant is no longer applicable.
- Preserve independently purchased service grants and course rights. Current card policy/qualification controls card limits only; disabling or downgrading a card must not revoke purchased rights. Admin may configure whether available card or purchased quota is consumed first; one operation consumes from one selected source, under the existing user lock and idempotency boundary.
- Reuse the existing learner service-entitlement view and server consumption boundary. Read endpoints do not provision grants. Successful service consumption provisions its required card grant atomically before recording usage. No generic browser endpoint may impersonate a service operation or accept another user's identity, remaining quota or provenance.
- System VI/EN applies to new labels, periods, remaining amounts and provenance explanations. Existing service names/content are not automatically translated. Reuse the approved GDS shell and native Admin components; use Stitch for substantial new controls/surfaces.
