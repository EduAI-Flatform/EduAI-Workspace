## Implemented contract

- Existing `membership.basis` accepts exactly `spend` or `topup`; there is no combined mode and no double counting of top-ups and purchases.
- Top-up classification reads the existing GDS top-up, wallet owner, immutable provider reconciliation and balanced `TOPUP_CREDIT` journal. It requires credited status, matching owner/reference/amount/currency/payment identity/receiving-account hashes and valid proof timestamps. Pending/cancelled, foreign-currency and out-of-window credits do not qualify. Reused proof/journal/collection evidence and inconsistent credited facts return unavailable.
- Qualification uses the Admin-configured VND thresholds and rolling calendar-month period. It measures verified historical credited top-ups, independently of current wallet balance; spending or withdrawal is not another top-up. Existing wallet journals have no top-up reversal workflow, and this patch does not invent one. New reversal sources must be incorporated before supporting such a workflow.
- Each result retains policy revision, evaluated time/window and a deterministic source digest; scans remain bounded and fail closed rather than truncate. The result contains no bank/account proof details.
- Policy defaults remain disabled. Removing the obsolete unsupported-source blocker only permits an Admin-approved, scheduled top-up classification policy. It does not activate any policy, payment admission, grant, refund, payout or card entitlement.
- Impact comparison loads both current/proposed source bases when changing spend/top-up. It separately reports tier changes and benefit changes, including benefit differences within the same tier and proposed disabling. Text benefits are explanatory policy metadata; they do not mutate historical course access.
- The existing Admin impact panel labels the qualifying basis correctly and shows benefit-change counts and localized before/after benefits. Fields are additive; older responses retain the spending label and show unavailable benefit totals as a dash.
